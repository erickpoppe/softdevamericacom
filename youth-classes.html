Classes are grouped for ages 6–11 and 12–17, with placement based on level as well as age.

Are classes online or in-person?

We offer both formats depending on the group.

My child already speaks some Spanish at home — is this still useful?

Yes, many of our students are heritage speakers building formal reading and writing skills.
