About Us

One Company, Four Ways to Help.

SoftDev of America is a technology and business solutions company. We build software — led by our flagship product, FastBooks — and we deliver the tax, document and education services that individuals and businesses actually need.

Our Story

Why SoftDev of America Exists

Software alone doesn’t file your taxes, notarize your documents, or teach your child calculus. Real problems need real technology and real expertise, working together.

SoftDev of America started as a technology company building FastBooks, our flagship accounting platform — and grew into a company that also delivers the professional services businesses and families rely on: taxes, documents, and education.

Our Mission

Combine Technology and Expertise So People Can Move Forward With Confidence.

Our Vision

To be the company people turn to for their software, their taxes, their documents and their education — under one trusted name.
